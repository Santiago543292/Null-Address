## About the Project

**Null Address** is a first-person horror experience focused on exploring creepy, abandoned locations while recording content for your YouTube channel. The goal is simple: go deeper into these forgotten places, capture the best footage, and climb the ranks as the ultimate horror YouTuber.

The game features atmospheric environments, a VHS/tape-style visual filter, and a focus on tension and exploration.

## Why I Made This

I made **Null Address** as a challenge to see how far I could push a complete game in just **3 days**. It was a test of rapid prototyping, scoping, and working under a tight deadline in Godot. The result is a small but atmospheric horror prototype that I’m proud of for the time spent.
